(04) — the small print, in large print

eight questions people actually ask

(1)can i cancel?+

yes, until seven days before, for free. after that we keep one night, and the fog keeps a little of your heart. it's fine. it gives it back.

(2)is there really no wifi?+

really. there's a landline at the desk for emergencies. it has a cord. people stand next to it for a while and then go back to the bath.

(3)what if i need my phone for work?+

then this week isn't for work. we can turn your phone on once a day, at 18:00, for ten minutes, at the desk, with tea. most people stop asking on the second day.

(4)how do i pay?+

at the desk, when you leave, by card or in cash. nothing is charged when you book here. nothing is sent from here at all, actually: we're fictional.

(5)i don't eat fish / meat / gluten…+

tell us at check in. the eleven small plates change every day anyway. nobody has ever left hungry; one guest left with the recipe for the miso.

(6)can i bring my dog?+

no, sorry. the house cat would never forgive us, and she's been here longer than the roof.

(7)what should i pack?+

warm socks, a book you've been meaning to read, nothing else. we have the yukata, the towels, the sandals, the umbrellas and the fog.

(8)will i actually see the mountain?+

maybe on the last morning, for an hour, if you're lucky. most people don't. most people say that was the point.
