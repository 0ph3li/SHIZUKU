    九十九a letter from behind the water

    to whoever found the nine that hide,

    i lived behind the ninety-ninth for eleven years. it is loud in there, and very quiet. the water falls in front of the door like a curtain, so nobody ever knocked.

    i didn't count the waterfalls. i counted the days i didn't need anything. by the end, the second number was bigger.

    you looked for something small in a lot of fog, and you didn't give up. that is most of what i know about being alive.

    the house will make you tea. tell them you found the letter. they'll pretend not to know what you mean, and then they'll give you the second bowl.

    the monkbehind no. 99, some years ago
