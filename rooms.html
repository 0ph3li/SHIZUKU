(02) — where you'll sleep

eleven rooms, four kinds
wood, paper, wool
and a lot of white outside

every room has a paper window,(1) a futon or a low bed, a kettle that's always warm,(2) and a waterfall you can hear but never see.(3) there's no tv and no clock.(4) you'll sleep like you were nine.(5)

  1. (1) outside it, mostly white.
  2. (2) genmaicha, sencha, or just hot water.
  3. (3) earplugs in the drawer, no judgement.
  4. (4) the fog changes colour. that's the clock.
  5. (5) or so says every guest, on the second night.
