(01) — 30 sec

falls basin

12°

fed straight from waterfall no. 3, through a bamboo pipe older than all of us. thirty seconds. scream if you need to; the falls are louder.

  1. (1) breathe out before you go in
  2. (2) shoulders under, count to thirty
  3. (3) out, slowly. you're new.
滝
bath-2.jpgthe lotus pool · 4:5

(02) — 20 min

lotus pool

36°

round, milky and warm. lotus flowers drift past like slow thoughts. don't chase them. lie back, ears under, and listen to the falls from inside the water.

  1. (1) ears under
  2. (2) let a flower touch you, don't touch it
  3. (3) leave before you fall asleep
蓮
bath-3.jpgthe fog room · 4:5

(03) — 12 min

fog room

48°

the fog from outside, brought in and heated. so thick you can't see your own hands, or anyone else's. people say things in here they've never said out loud.

  1. (1) sit on the lowest step first
  2. (2) twelve minutes, no more
  3. (3) then the falls basin again
霧
bath-4.jpgthe stone pool · 4:5

(04) — 15 min

stone pool

41°

open-air, under the eaves. in winter the snow lands on your hair and melts before it reaches the water. hot body, cold face: the best feeling we know.

  1. (1) towel on your head, not in the water
  2. (2) look up
  3. (3) say nothing

(05) — after 23:00

night garden

39°

lanterns, moss, the falls somewhere in the dark. the quietest place we own. it stays open until 02:00, or until the last person floats out.

  1. (1) follow the lanterns
  2. (2) stay as long as you like
  3. (3) blow out the last one
