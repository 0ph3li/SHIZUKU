(02) — what a ritual is, up here

six of them
book at the desk, or don't
most start when you arrive

a ritual is just something done slowly, on purpose,(1) more than once. up here we have six. one is a walk,(2) one is tea, one is lying down and listening to water.(3) none of them will fix you.(4) all of them will help.(5)

  1. (1) like brushing your hair, but nicer.
  2. (2) in the fog. you'll be fine. probably.
  3. (3) the hardest one, it turns out.
  4. (4) you're not broken. you're tired.
  5. (5) or so says the fog.

(03) — six rituals

timeprice

60 minincluded

the fog walk

the cedar boardwalk, into the fog, to the third waterfall and back. no talking, no phone, no destination. you'll hear the falls long before you see them.

  1. (1) wool socks, wooden sandals
  2. (2) follow the rope, not your thoughts
  3. (3) turn back at the third waterfall

every morning, 07:00 · meet at the door

霧

45 min€ 44

the snow bath

an open-air pool in the snow, a thick wooden pillar, white trees all around. a little wooden tub floats over with hot tea in it. hot water, cold face, nobody says a word.

  1. (1) wait for snow (november to march)
  2. (2) wait for the tea to float over
  3. (3) sink to the chin

whenever it snows · we'll ring a bell

雪

60 min€ 58

the night room

after 23:00, a private cedar bath under the trees: wet stone, one lantern, a candle on the water, the falls somewhere outside. you can cry in here; the water won't tell anyone.

  1. (1) one lantern, lit for you
  2. (2) sandals off at the stone
  3. (3) blow the lantern out when you leave

23:00 to 02:00 · one guest at a time

夜

30 minincluded

the guardians at dawn

three small stone guardians sit in the moss below the falls. sit down next to them at dawn, while the fog turns pink. they've been meditating for four hundred years; they'll show you how, mostly by not moving.

  1. (1) up at 05:30, we'll knock
  2. (2) a blanket and a cushion
  3. (3) copy the one in the middle

every dawn · about four minutes of pink

石

45 min€ 30

the tea hour

matcha, whisked by someone who has done it every day for thirty years, on a long tray by the window over the forest. you will want to say something. don't.

  1. (1) hold the bowl with both hands
  2. (2) turn it twice, towards you
  3. (3) three sips, the last one loud

16:00 · the long window, ground floor

茶

30 minfree, like air

listening to the falls

waterfall no. 3 falls in eleven steps through the forest. lie on the wooden deck in front of it, a warm cloth on your eyes, and listen. that's it. it's the hardest ritual we have; most people last eight minutes the first time.

  1. (1) lie down, cloth on your eyes
  2. (2) count the sounds inside the sound
  3. (3) lose count

any time · the deck is always there
